![[Page 0-1.png]]# `/Sticker-Book` Command

## Parameters

The `/Sticker-Book` command has two optional parameters:

### `user:username`

Append `user:username` to view another user's sticker book.

### `hidden:true`

Append `hidden:true` to display the sticker book  only to you. The result also grants you the control to dismiss the message.
